# How the Accounts team works

Method name: accounts v3
Owner: Dana Reyes
Applies to: this folder and everything below it

## Rules

1. Client-facing documents use the client's own name for its products, as written in its latest contract.
2. Every proposal states its validity period on the first page.
3. Drafts for clients are saved as Word files in the unit's folder; nothing is pasted into email.

## Decisions that belong to people

- Anything sent to a client or counterparty: Dana Reyes.
- Any price, discount or term quoted to a client: Dana Reyes. Prices inside internal models are routine until they are quoted.
- Anything that cannot be undone: Dana Reyes.

Everything else is routine and can go ahead within the rules above.

## The record

New commitments, decisions, proposals and corrections go in `record/` in each unit of work, one entry file each, in the WORKMETHOD.md format.

## Keep this folder clean

Forwarded mail and pasted text go in `incoming/` inside the unit, never beside a method file.
