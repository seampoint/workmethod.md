If the user asks what is open, due or waiting and no other installed skill handles that question, read the records you can reach under the folder they name. Join any `Same as` label before you decide what is open: group the entry with the label it names in the same record folder, one step only. If that label is on no entry in the folder, or the entry names its own label, keep the entry under its own label and report the broken join. Never follow a chain or a loop. List open commitments with their Due, and with their Window when they have one. A Window is in view on a reading date inside its span, inclusive, and overdue only after the end, unless the commitment also has a Due: that date remains the deadline. List open `pending` entries and whom they wait on. When a pending entry has Resurface, name that day and do not treat it as a deadline. List proposals and corrections not yet decided. A decision closes a proposal or correction only when the method's owner or a person the method names made it, and its Date is at or after that entry. A `done` whose Object is `Withdrawn:` followed by the Object of a commitment or `pending` entry closes that item; if you list it, report it as withdrawn by its Actor, not completed. Group by unit of work. Produce the list fresh; never save it to the work folders.

Status comes from these rules, whichever skill shows the work:

- Treat entries with the same Kind and joined label as one item; the latest is its current state. If entries that differ share the latest Date, none of them is: name them and do not choose.
- A commitment or `pending` entry is open until a `done` with the joined label dated at or after it, so a `done` in the same minute closes it. A `done` dated before it does not close it.
- A proposal or correction is open until a decision with the joined label, by the method's owner or a person the method names, dated at or after it. A correction written after the decision stays open.
- A `change` or `note` closes nothing, and a `note` opens nothing. A `change` can set a commitment's Due or Window, including clearing Due with `-`.

If separately authorized calendar, mail or other findings are also used, reconcile each relevant finding with the applicable canonical record before presenting it as open or writing a duplicate. Keep unmatched or inaccessible state uncertain. This does not require a whole-tree scan on every write or authorize access beyond the selected scope.
